# ShopEasy Payment Issues and Billing Policy

ShopEasy employs bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and strict PCI-DSS compliance standards to guarantee the safety of every financial transaction.

## Accepted Payment Methods
Customers can complete purchases using the following options:
- Major Credit and Debit Cards: **Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover**.
- Digital Wallets: **PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay**.
- **ShopEasy Gift Cards** and online store credits.
- Please note: We **do not accept** Cash on Delivery (COD), personal checks, money orders, direct wire transfers, or cryptocurrency payments.

## Resolving Payment Failures and Declined Transactions
If your payment is declined during checkout, this is frequently caused by one of the following reasons:
1. **Billing Address Mismatch:** The billing postal code or street address entered does not precisely match the records on file with your card issuer.
2. **Security Verification (3D Secure / OTP):** Failure to enter the SMS verification code or approve the bank mobile app push alert within the time limit.
3. **Card Limits or Fraud Filters:** Your card issuing institution flagged the e-commerce transaction as unusual activity or the charge exceeds your daily card spending limit.
4. **Card Expiration or CVV Error:** Check that the 3-digit security code (or 4 digits for American Express) and expiration month/year are entered correctly.

### Recommended Steps to Complete Your Order:
- Re-check your billing details carefully in checkout step 2.
- Switch to another payment method, such as PayPal or Apple Pay.
- Call your financial institution's customer service number (found on the reverse side of your payment card) and authorize charges from "ShopEasy Commerce".

## Duplicate Charges and Pending Authorizations
If you notice multiple identical charges on your online banking feed immediately after checkout:
- In 99% of cases, one of these entries is a temporary authorization hold placed by the card processor to verify funds.
- Temporary holds are automatically released by your bank within **3 to 5 business days** and will never settle as an actual charge.
- If both charges transition from "Pending" to "Posted" or "Cleared" status on your bank statement, contact ShopEasy support immediately with your statement extract and order confirmation ID for an immediate refund of the duplicate amount.

## Using ShopEasy Gift Cards
Gift card codes are 16 alphanumeric characters. You may apply up to **2 gift cards per order**. If the card balance is less than the total cart amount, you must cover the remaining balance with another valid payment method.
