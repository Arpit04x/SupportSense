# ShopEasy Order Cancellation Policy

We understand that circumstances change and you may need to alter or cancel a purchase. Because our fulfillment system is highly automated, please review our strict timeline rules below.

## Self-Service Cancellation Window
- Customers can cancel an order autonomously within **60 minutes** of order submission.
- To cancel within this window:
  1. Go to **My Orders** in your ShopEasy account.
  2. Select the recent order number.
  3. Click the red **Cancel Order** button and select a cancellation reason.
  4. You will receive an immediate on-screen notification and an automated email confirming the cancellation.

## After the 60-Minute Cancellation Window
- Once **60 minutes** have elapsed from payment submission, your order is automatically transmitted to our robotic distribution center for automated picking, sorting, and packaging.
- At this stage, customer service representatives **cannot halt or intercept** the shipment.
- If your order has progressed past the 60-minute window, please wait until the parcel arrives at your delivery address, then initiate a standard return via our **Returns and Refunds Portal**.

## Modifying Shipping Address or Order Items
- **Adding or Changing Products:** Items cannot be added or replaced on an active order. You must cancel the order (if within the 60-minute cutoff) and submit a new order with the correct items.
- **Updating Delivery Address:** Address corrections are only possible within the **60-minute** window via the order details page. Once dispatched, carrier reroutes are not guaranteed and may incur carrier re-consignment fees of up to **$15.00**.

## Automatic Inventory Out-of-Stock Cancellations
- While our inventory counts are synchronized in real-time, unexpected inventory discrepancies may occasionally arise.
- If an item in your order is found to be out of stock:
  - We will immediately notify you via email.
  - The unavailable item will be cancelled, and all remaining items in your order will be shipped as scheduled.
  - A full refund for the cancelled item (including applicable sales tax and proportional shipping) is automatically processed to your original payment method within **24 hours**.

## Cancellation Refunds and Pre-Authorization Releases
- If you cancel an order within the eligible 60-minute window, any pending payment authorization hold is voided immediately.
- Depending on your card issuer or banking institution, the authorization record will disappear from your statement within **2 to 5 business days**.
