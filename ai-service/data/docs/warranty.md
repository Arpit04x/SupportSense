# ShopEasy Limited Warranty and Protection Plans

ShopEasy stands firmly behind the durability and performance of our merchandise. We offer comprehensive warranty coverage on all electronics, smart devices, and home appliances.

## 1-Year Standard Manufacturer Limited Warranty
- Every electronic device, power tool, and smart household appliance sold by ShopEasy comes with a complimentary **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty**, effective from the date of product delivery.
- **What is Covered:**
  - Hardware defects arising from materials or manufacturing workmanship.
  - Component failures under normal, intended household usage.
  - Internal motor, circuitry, and power supply breakdowns.
  - Unresponsive touchscreens, control dials, or internal sensor malfunctions.
- **What is Excluded from Coverage:**
  - Accidental drops, physical impact cracks, and crushed screens.
  - Liquid submersion, water ingress, or chemical exposure (unless specifically marked IP68 waterproof and used within certified depth limits).
  - Normal cosmetic wear and tear, superficial scuffs, scratches, or color fading.
  - Unauthorized repairs, tampering, or modifications executed by third-party technicians.
  - Damage resulting from electrical power surges or using uncertified power adapters.

## ShopEasy Care+ Extended Protection
Customers may upgrade to **ShopEasy Care+** within **30 days** of original hardware purchase:
- **Available Terms:** 2-Year or 3-Year comprehensive protection extensions.
- **Accidental Damage Protection (ADH):** Covers accidental drops, spills, and cracked screens with a low copay/deductible of **$25.00** per claim (maximum 2 claims per rolling 12-month period).
- **Battery Replacement Guarantee:** Includes one complimentary battery replacement if the original battery capacity degrades below 75% health.

## Filing a Warranty Claim: Step-by-Step
To request warranty service, follow these instructions:
1. Have your **10-digit ShopEasy Order Number** and device serial number ready.
2. Go to **shopeasy.com/warranty-portal** and select "File New Claim".
3. Provide a clear description of the defect and upload up to 3 high-resolution photos or a short video demonstrating the malfunction.
4. Our engineering diagnostics team reviews all warranty claims within **48 business hours**.
5. Upon approval, we provide a prepaid insured shipping label. We will either repair your device or ship a brand-new replacement unit within **7 to 10 business days**. If the product has been discontinued or is unavailable, full store credit is issued.
