# ShopEasy Shipping and Delivery Guidelines

ShopEasy partners with premier global carriers including FedEx, UPS, and DHL to deliver your purchases safely and swiftly.

## Shipping Methods, Speeds, and Costs
We offer three shipping options for domestic orders within the United States:
1. **Standard Domestic Shipping:**
   - **Delivery Window:** **3 to 5 business days**.
   - **Cost:** **$4.99** flat rate, or **FREE** on all orders with a subtotal of **$50.00 or higher** (before taxes and after discounts).
2. **Expedited 2-Day Shipping:**
   - **Delivery Window:** Exactly **2 business days**.
   - **Cost:** **$12.99** flat rate regardless of cart size.
3. **Overnight Priority Express:**
   - **Delivery Window:** **Next business day** delivery.
   - **Order Cut-Off:** Orders must be placed before **1:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)** Monday through Friday.
   - **Cost:** **$24.99** flat rate.

## Warehouse Processing Times
- Orders placed on business days (Monday through Friday) are processed and packed within **24 to 48 hours**.
- Orders placed on weekends or observed national holidays will begin processing on the following business day.

## Tracking Your Order
As soon as your shipping label is generated, an automated dispatch email is sent containing your unique carrier tracking code. You can monitor package milestones at any time by visiting **shopeasy.com/track** and entering your order number and billing zip code. Please allow up to **24 hours** for tracking scans to display initial updates.

## International Deliveries
ShopEasy currently ships to Canada and the United Kingdom in addition to the US:
- **Canada Delivery:** Takes **7 to 10 business days**. Duties and import taxes are pre-calculated at checkout with DDP (Delivered Duty Paid).
- **United Kingdom Delivery:** Takes **8 to 14 business days**. Value Added Tax (VAT) is collected during checkout.
- We currently do not offer standard shipping to other international destinations.

## Delayed, Lost, or Stolen Packages
- If your tracking status has not updated for **5 consecutive business days**, please notify our support team immediately.
- If tracking indicates "Delivered" but you cannot locate the package, please check around your porch, building lobby, or with neighbors, and wait **24 hours** as carriers occasionally scan parcels prematurely.
- Claims for lost or missing shipments must be reported within **14 calendar days** of the marked delivery date for a full replacement or refund.
