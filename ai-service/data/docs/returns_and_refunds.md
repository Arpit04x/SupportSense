# ShopEasy Returns and Refunds Policy

Welcome to the ShopEasy Returns and Refunds guide. We strive to ensure your complete satisfaction with every purchase. Please review our detailed policies below.

## Standard Return Window
- **Standard Items:** Customers can return eligible products within **30 days** of the confirmed delivery date.
- **Electronics and Refurbished Devices:** These categories must be returned within **14 days** of the delivery date.
- All returned merchandise must be unused, unwashed, and in its original retail packaging, complete with all included accessories, manuals, tags, and proof of purchase (such as an order confirmation email or packing slip).

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items are strictly non-returnable:
- Perishable goods (food items, beverages, and flowers).
- Downloadable software products, digital licenses, and opened media discs.
- Customized or personalized items (such as monogrammed apparel or custom engravings).
- Personal care, hygiene, and intimate apparel products for health safety reasons.
- ShopEasy physical and digital gift cards.

## How to Initiate a Return
Returning an item is quick and convenient:
1. Sign in to your ShopEasy account at `shopeasy.com` and open **My Orders**.
2. Locate the relevant order and click **Initiate Return**.
3. Select the items you wish to return and provide the reason for return.
4. Download and print the prepaid return shipping label.
5. Pack the items securely in durable packaging, affix the return label to the outside, and drop the package off at any authorized FedEx or UPS drop-off location.

## Return Shipping Costs
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items:** Return shipping is **100% free**. ShopEasy covers all carrier expenses.
- **Change-of-Mind Returns:** A flat fee of **$5.99** will be automatically deducted from your final refund total to cover return postage and warehouse handling.

## Refund Processing and Timelines
Once your return shipment arrives at our central fulfillment warehouse, our inspection team inspects the items within **3 business days**. Once approved:
- **Credit and Debit Cards:** Refunds take **5 to 7 business days** to reflect on your banking statement.
- **PayPal:** Refunds are credited within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **Store Credit:** Issued immediately to your ShopEasy wallet upon return inspection completion.
- Note that initial shipping charges from the original purchase are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopEasy fulfillment error.
