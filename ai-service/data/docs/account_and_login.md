# ShopEasy Account Management and Login Troubleshooting

Your ShopEasy account gives you instant access to order history, shipment tracking, wishlist items, and exclusive member discounts.

## Creating an Account and Password Rules
To register for a new ShopEasy account:
- Visit `shopeasy.com/register` and enter your full name and a valid email address.
- Your password must meet these security requirements:
  - At least **8 characters** in total length.
  - Contain at least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
  - Contain at least one numerical digit (0-9).
  - Contain at least one special symbol (e.g., ! @ # $ % ^ & *).

## Password Reset Instructions
If you cannot remember your account password or wish to reset it:
1. Navigate to the login page and click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Input the email address registered with your ShopEasy account and submit the form.
3. You will receive an email containing a secure password reset link within **2 to 5 minutes**. Please check your Spam or Junk folder if you do not see it in your Inbox.
4. The password reset link is single-use and remains valid for exactly **60 minutes**. If it expires, you must submit a new request.

## Account Lockout and Security
To prevent brute-force attacks and protect customer data, ShopEasy implements automatic security lockouts:
- Entering an incorrect password **5 consecutive times** will temporarily lock your account for **30 minutes**.
- You can either wait out the 30-minute lockout period or click "Forgot Password" to immediately unlock your profile by setting a new secure password.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
We strongly urge all customers to enable Two-Factor Authentication:
- Go to **Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**.
- Choose between **SMS Verification Code** or **Authenticator App** (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password).
- Once configured, you will be prompted for a 6-digit one-time passcode upon logging in from any unrecognized device or web browser.

## Changing Profile Information and Account Deletion
- **Shipping and Billing Addresses:** Easily updated under **My Account > Saved Addresses**.
- **Email Address Changes:** Updating your primary email address requires confirmation via a verification link sent to both your existing email and your proposed new email address.
- **Account Closure:** To permanently delete your account, visit **Account Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete My Account**. Please note that account deletion permanently voids any accrued ShopEasy reward points and store credits, and cannot be undone. Active orders must be delivered or cancelled prior to account closure.
